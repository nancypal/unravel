    Privacy

    This site doesn't use accounts or cookies and doesn't collect personal information about you. When you search, the server asks Ravelry for matching patterns and projects and passes the results to your browser. It keeps only short-lived, in-memory counts (like how many photo projects a pattern has) so it doesn't ask Ravelry the same thing repeatedly; nothing is saved permanently. Clicking a photo, pattern or designer takes you to that page on Ravelry. Like most websites, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses), and fonts load from Google Fonts.
